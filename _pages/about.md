### Welcome!
I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Economics at the University of Chicago, focusing on topics relating to development economics, labor economics, and environmental economics. My projects focus on small firm performance, firm decision making, and job search in developing markets.

**I am on the 2026-2027 academic job market.**

Feel free to contact me [here](mailto:kuloszewski@uchicago.edu). You can find my CV [here]({{ '/assets/pdf/cv2026aug.pdf' | relative_url }}).
